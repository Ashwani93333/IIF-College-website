Genesis & Mission

Academic Distinction Rooted in Real Corporate Excellence

IIF College of Commerce & Management Studies (IIF CCMS) is affiliated to Chaudhary Charan Singh (CCS) University, Meerut. IIF College of Commerce & Management Studies was founded under the aegis of Jyoti Foundation, a Public Charitable Trust duly registered U/S 12 of the Income Tax Act 1961. It is an integral part of the prestigious Indian Institute of Finance Group (founded in 1987).

The main campus of the College is spread over an expansive area of 6.47 acres in Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida (Delhi NCR), housing Academic Departments, Central Library, Board Meeting Rooms, Faculty Staff Rooms, Air-Conditioned Classrooms, Computer Laboratories, and dedicated Common Rooms for Boys and Girls.

  • Eminent Faculty: Bestowed with distinguished faculty educated or taught at Ivy League and top Indian institutions like SRCC, FMS, Delhi University, IIT Delhi, Columbia University USA, and Cleveland State University.
  • Tremendous Research Resources: CCMS students enjoy access to the world-class IIF Central Library with over 55,000 volumes, 1,000 national and international journals, and global financial databases.
  • Advanced IT Centre: High-speed Wi-Fi enabled campus with computer centers equipped with 20+ dedicated workstations open for students, faculty, and research scholars.
  • Holistic Career Pathways: Ample opportunities to pursue admissions into top MBA colleges in India and abroad, pursue CA, CS, M.Com, or enter high-growth careers in Banking, Finance, and Civil Services.
